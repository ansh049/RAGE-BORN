# Terminal and OS Control

RAGE is designed to be your complete AI operating companion for Linux, giving you unparalleled control over your entire OS through a natural language interface.

## Core Capabilities
- **Terminal Commands**: RAGE can execute terminal commands autonomously based on your instructions. It features multi-terminal tabs to run multiple named terminal sessions in parallel.
- **OS Control**: RAGE can act as a system administrator, managing your system, installing packages, and performing disk operations.
- **Background Tasks**: Long-running operations can be sent to the background, and RAGE will keep track of their progress while you do other things.
- **Safety First**: With built-in dangerous command detection, RAGE stops and asks for your confirmation before executing destructive commands like `rm -rf`, disk operations (`dd`), filesystem formatting, reboots, or firewall changes.
