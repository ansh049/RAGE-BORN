# Browser Automation and Interactions

RAGE features a Playwright-powered built-in browser engine that can interact with the web just like a human does.

## Key Browser Features
- **Persistent Storage**: RAGE stores cookies, sessions, and bookmarks in `~/.rage/browser_profile/`. You only need to log in once!
- **Social Media Browsing**: You can instruct RAGE to open LinkedIn, X (Twitter), or other social media, scroll through your feeds, and check your notifications.
- **Form Filling & Interaction**: Ask RAGE to navigate to a page and fill out forms, click buttons, and interact with the UI.
- **Screenshots & Context**: Take screenshots of a page to analyze its UI or have RAGE read the DOM and summarize the article for you.
